How to play
  • Type your username and pick a level.
  • Tap a tile to open it. The number shows how many bombs touch that tile.
  • Open every safe tile. Hit a bomb and you lose!
  • Touch and hold a tile (or right-click) to plant a flag 🚩 on a suspected bomb. Hold again to remove it.
  • Tap an opened number to highlight the closed tiles around it.
  • Buy 🔍 Spy Glasses in the Shop. Tap Spy, then a closed tile, to see through it: ✅ safe or 💣 bomb. (Open a tile first.)
  • Beat the clock! When time is almost out, the panic alarm starts: the last 10 seconds on Easy, the last 20 on Medium and Hard.
  • Win to earn coins 🪙 and spend them in the Shop on new backgrounds.
